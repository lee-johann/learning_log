Four noble truths
- 4 truths (四谛di4/四真谛)
	- Dukkha ([baidu](https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E8%8B%A6%E8%B0%9B/11053362) 苦谛): 苦 suffering , unsatisfactory, discontent, stress
	- cause of dukkha 集谛: 心念 craving (tanha) for sentuality, states of becoming, states of no becoming
	- cessation (relinquishment) of dukkha 灭谛
	- practice that leads to cessation 道谛: noble eightfold path (view, resolve, speech, action, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, concentration)
- samsara 论回 - the cycle of life
	- ignorance of 4 noble truths binds you to samsara
- karma 业 (or 羯jie2磨)- universal law of cause and effect
	-  ignorance of karma causes an aimless existence: happy one moment, despair the next
- goal state is nirvana 涅nie4槃pan2 or 圆寂(transcending ignorance, craving, suffering, karma into freedom)
- why the eightfold path is necessary: the mindi s stubborn, with habitual tendencies that need to be uprooted over time (with habit)
	- ex. generosity erodes the habitual tendency towards craving
